Jarvis

A personal desktop assistant by Barnabas Juhasz

Jarvis is a personal, single-user desktop assistant that Barnabas Juhasz builds and uses for himself. It runs on his own computer and helps him browse his notes and read his own Gmail and Google Health data.

Jarvis is not offered to the public. There are no other users, accounts, downloads or sign-ups.
